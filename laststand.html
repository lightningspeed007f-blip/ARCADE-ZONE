CONTROLS

PHONE (LANDSCAPE)

  • Left side — touch & drag to walk. Push all the way up to run.
  • Right side — drag to look around.
  • FIRE — hold to shoot; drag on it to aim while firing.
  • AIM · RELOAD · USE · CROUCH · RUN · SWAP
  • BOMB — light and throw a desi bomb where you are looking (aim up to throw further).
  • EAT — eats food from your bag (the only way to heal).
  • TORCH — flashlight (people can see you from further away).
  • DRIVE — appears next to the jeep or the pickup. Then ◀ ▶ steer, GAS, BRAKE (hold to reverse), EXIT. The left stick also drives.
  • Minimap (top left on phones) — tap it to open the full map of Jais.
  • Settings → EDIT BUTTON LAYOUT to move buttons.

KEYBOARD & MOUSE

  • WASD move · mouse look
  • LMB fire · RMB aim
  • R reload · E open / take / search
  • Shift sprint · C crouch
  • F torch · H eat · Q/wheel switch weapon
  • 1–7 weapon slots · V fists
  • G / B throw a desi bomb · G next to a car gets in
  • G / E get in / out of the jeep or the pickup
  • Driving: WS gas / brake-reverse · AD steer · Space handbrake · mouse looks around
  • M map · Esc pause

SURVIVAL

  • One life. No health regeneration.
  • Gunshots carry far — zombies and looters come to the sound.
  • Headshots matter. Ammo is scarce.
  • Locked doors can be kicked in (loud). Zombies can break doors too.
  • Desi bombs: about 2 seconds of fuse, a big blast, and you are not immune. A bomb that lands makes a noise zombies go to look at. Looters throw them too — watch for the fizzing spark.
  • Zombies will not enter the Maa Kamakhya Mandir compound. Looters will.
  • Something enormous sleeps beside the tracks west of Guru Gorakhnath Dham. Let it sleep — until you can't.
  • The Amethi road ends at a highway sign. Beyond it: a forest road, hounds, and a dead city with a metro back to Jais City station. The armoured pickup parked on that road is the way to go.

CREDITS

JAIS: LAST STAND — a fictional survival game set in a playable, imagined version of Jais, Uttar Pradesh: Wahabganj, Naugazi Tiraha, Alia Market, Maa Kamakhya Mandir, the bus adda, Jais City (formerly Kasimpur Halt) and Guru Gorakhnath Dham station.


All buildings, textures, signboards and sounds are generated in code for this game. No map imagery was copied. Shop names are invented. Places are inspired by the real town; the layout is a game version, not a survey map.
